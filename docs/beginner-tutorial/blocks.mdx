# Blocks and Indentation

Bujji follows Python-style block structure. Blocks are identified by
indentation.

That means spacing matters.

## What is a block?

A block is a group of statements that belong together. In Bujji, a line ending
with `:` can introduce a block, and the lines inside that block are indented.

<BujjiCode code={`condition ithe:
    "condition nijam" anicheppu
    "this line is inside the block" anicheppu`} />

The two indented lines belong to the condition.

## Indentation shows ownership

Compare these two examples.

Inside the block:

<BujjiCode code={`age = 20 bujji

age > 18 ithe:
    "adult" anicheppu
    "inside condition block" anicheppu`} />

Outside the block:

<BujjiCode code={`age = 20 bujji

age > 18 ithe:
    "adult" anicheppu

"this runs after the condition block" anicheppu`} />

The blank line does not matter as much as indentation. The important idea is:
indented statements belong to the block.

## Keep indentation consistent

Use the same number of spaces for every line in the same block.

Good:

<BujjiCode code={`marks = 90 bujji

marks > 35 ithe:
    "pass" anicheppu
    "well done" anicheppu`} />

Confusing:

<BujjiCode code={`marks = 90 bujji

marks > 35 ithe:
    "pass" anicheppu
  "well done" anicheppu`} />

When indentation is inconsistent, it becomes unclear which statement belongs to
the block.
